DNS lookup

Every record type for a hostname, with time-to-live values, answered live by Google Public DNS and Cloudflare.

Enter a hostname above. Results show here, grouped by record type.

Which record does what

TypeUse
A / AAAAThe IPv4 / IPv6 address a name points to. Your website lives here.
CNAMEAn alias to another hostname. Common for www and for hosted services.
MXWhich servers receive your email, lowest number first.
NSWhich nameservers are authoritative for the zone.
TXTPolicies and proofs: SPF, DMARC (at _dmarc), site verification codes.
CAAWhich certificate authorities may issue certificates for the name.
SOAThe zone’s primary server, contact and timers.
DSThe DNSSEC link held by the parent zone. Present means signing is switched on at the registry.
HTTPSHints that let browsers connect faster and over HTTP/3.

Checking a change has gone live

DNS answers are cached for the number of seconds in their TTL. After you change a record, resolvers keep serving the old answer until that time runs out. Tick Compare to ask two large public resolvers at once: if they agree with your new value, most of the internet will soon follow. Lower the TTL to 300 a day before planned moves.
