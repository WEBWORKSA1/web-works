Why these checks

Most launch problems are boring: an expired card lets the domain lapse, a missing DMARC record sends the welcome email to spam, a slow hero image costs the first visitors. The list starts with the domain because everything else depends on it, and ends with revenue because ad networks and partners look for the basics before they say yes.
