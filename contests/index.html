Official rules

  1. Organizer. The contest is run by Web.Works. Contact us through the contact page.
  2. No purchase necessary. Entry is free. Buying anything does not improve your chances.
  3. Eligibility. Open to individuals aged 18 or over who have reached the age of majority where they live, where the contest is not prohibited or restricted by law. Void where prohibited. Employees and contractors of Web.Works and of that round’s sponsor, and their households, cannot win.
  4. Entry period. Each round opens on the first day of the month and closes on the last day of the month at 23:59 UTC. Late entries are not judged.
  5. How to enter. Submit up to five names per round through the form on this page. One submission per person per round; additional submissions replace earlier ones.
  6. Judging. A panel chosen by Web.Works scores entries using the published criteria. This is a contest of skill; chance plays no part. The panel’s decision is final.
  7. Prize. The prize for each round is shown on this page when the round opens and is funded by sponsors or supporters. If no eligible entry meets the brief, no prize is awarded and the prize rolls into the next round. Prizes are not transferable. Cash prizes are paid by bank transfer or PayPal within 30 days of the winner confirming eligibility. Winners are responsible for any taxes. Canadian winners may be asked to answer a time-limited skill-testing question.
  8. Winner notice. The winner is contacted by email and must reply within 14 days, or an alternate winner may be chosen. The winner’s first name and winning name are announced in the daily brief.
  9. Your entries. You confirm the names are your own ideas. Names generally cannot be owned until registered or trademarked, so you agree that the brief’s owner, Web.Works and the sponsor may use any name submitted, and that only the winning entry receives a prize. Do not register domains for names you submit to a round while it is open.
  10. Privacy. Entry details are used to run the contest and contact you, as described in our privacy policy.
  11. Changes. Web.Works may cancel, suspend or modify a round if fraud, technical failure or any other factor impairs its integrity, and will post any change on this page.
