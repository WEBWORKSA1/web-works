Raising a trademark or copyright concern

If you believe something on this site infringes your rights, send a notice through our contact form with:

  1. the work or mark you believe is infringed, and proof that you own it or act for the owner;

  3. your name, organization and contact details;
  4. a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent or the law; and
  5. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate, with your physical or electronic signature.

We review every notice promptly and remove or correct material where appropriate. If your material was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you can send a counter-notice the same way.
