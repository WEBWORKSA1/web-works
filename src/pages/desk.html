What the Desk watches

  • Expiry: days left, flagged at 60, 30 and 7 days, and after expiry.
  • Status codes: redemption and pending-delete mean a name is leaving its owner.
  • Registrar and nameserver changes since your last refresh: often the first sign of a transfer, a sale or a hijack.
  • Targets that free up: a wanted name that is no longer registered.

The Desk checks when you open it and press refresh. For checks every hour with alerts to your phone and a human on call, use managed monitoring.
