Choosing a route

Direct deal with escrow is the cheapest route when you can find and talk to the owner yourself. The escrow agent holds the money until the domain has moved, which protects both sides.

Buy-side broker costs more, but keeps your identity hidden. When a well-known company asks for a name, the price tends to rise; a broker avoids that and handles the back-and-forth.

Marketplace sale suits sellers with a listed price. The marketplace handles payment and transfer, and takes a commission.

Lease to own spreads the price over months. Read who holds the domain until the final payment and what happens if a payment is missed.
