Who we are

This policy covers the website at web.works (“Web.Works”, “we”, “us”). You can reach us, including the person responsible for personal information, through our contact form.

What we collect and why

Information you send us

When you use a form (project planner, contact, advertising inquiry, contest entry, support pledge or talent network) we receive what you type, plus the page you sent it from, the referring page and any campaign tags in the link that brought you here. We use it to answer you, provide what you asked for, run the contest or process your pledge. Forms are delivered to our inbox by a third-party form relay service (FormSubmit), which processes the submission only to deliver it.

Tool data stays in your browser

Your Desk watchlist, targets, shortlists, checklist progress and recent lookups are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data or using the Desk’s Clear button deletes them.

Lookups go directly to public services

When you look up a domain or DNS record, your browser contacts the relevant registry’s RDAP service (or the rdap.org redirector), Google Public DNS and Cloudflare DNS directly. Those services see your IP address and the name you looked up, under their own privacy policies. We do not see or store your lookups.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We show ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where the law requires it, visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before personalized ads are shown.

Videos, fonts and hosting

  • YouTube: video players load only when you press play, using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode. After that, Google’s privacy policy applies to the player.
  • Google Fonts: the site’s typeface is loaded from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security under GitHub’s privacy statement.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell personal information.
  • We don’t create accounts or passwords.
  • We don’t record which domains you look up.

Legal bases (EEA and UK visitors)

We process form submissions to take steps you ask for before a contract and to perform it, contest entries and talent-network details with your consent, and security and site-improvement data on the basis of our legitimate interests. Personalized advertising relies on your consent where required.

How long we keep it

  • Inquiries and project requests: up to 24 months after our last contact, longer if needed for a contract or legal obligation.
  • Contest entries: 12 months after the round closes; winners’ names as published.
  • Talent network: until you ask us to remove you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent at any time. California residents have the right to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information; we do not sell it, and you can opt out of personalized ads through Google Ads Settings. Residents of Canada, including Quebec, have rights of access and correction and may file complaints with the relevant privacy commissioner. To use any right, contact us through the contact form. We answer within 30 days.

Children

The site isn’t directed to children under 16, and the contest is open only to adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from children.

International transfers and security

Our service providers may process information in Canada, the United States and other countries. We use providers that protect data in transit with encryption and limit who can read submissions.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the effective date at the top. Significant changes will be noted in the daily brief.
