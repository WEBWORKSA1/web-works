How to judge a generated name

  1. Say it once. If someone hearing it can’t spell it, you will spend money explaining it.
  2. Check the meaning. Search the word in the languages of your main markets.
  3. Check trademarks. Search the USPTO, CIPO (Canada) or EUIPO databases in your class of goods and services before you commit.
  4. Check the history. Look the domain up in the Wayback Machine; a spam past can follow it.
  5. Check social handles. Matching handles are a bonus, not a requirement.
