Services

Four ways we take a domain problem off your hands. Each starts with a free written plan, so you know the route and the cost before anything is committed.

Monitoring & brand protection

We watch your domains and your brand, so a lapsed renewal, a hijacked nameserver or a phishing lookalike reaches a person before it reaches your customers.

  • Expiry, registrar, status and nameserver checks on every domain you own
  • Lookalike and typo scans of your brand names, plus new-certificate watch
  • DNS and email-authentication drift alerts (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, DNSSEC)
  • Takedown and UDRP support when a lookalike turns hostile
  • A quarterly portfolio report: what to renew, drop, protect or sell

Good fit if

You own more than a handful of domains, your domain sends email to customers, or your brand is a target for impersonation.

Acquisition

The name you want is taken. We find out who owns it, approach them without revealing who you are, and negotiate within the ceiling you set.

  1. Brief. You tell us the name, your ceiling and your deadline.
  2. Research. Ownership history, the owner’s other names and their likely price expectations.
  3. Approach. Anonymous outreach and negotiation, reported to you at each step.
  4. Close. Payment through a licensed escrow service; we stay on until the domain is in your account.

If the name can’t be had at your price, we bring you the best alternatives we found along the way.

Fees

A small research fee to start, then a success fee only if the deal closes. Your quote states both before work begins.

Naming & branding

A shortlist of names you can actually get, each tested for how it sounds, what it means and whether it’s safe to trademark.

  • A naming brief built from a short interview about your business
  • Around twenty screened candidates with available or attainable domains
  • Pronunciation, meaning and basic trademark-conflict screening for finalists
  • Matching extensions and defensive registrations for the winner
  • Optional logo direction and a one-page brand starter

Consulting & builds

Practical help for owners of domains and the sites on them.

  • Portfolio review: renewal costs, names to drop, names to sell and how to price them
  • DNS and email hardening: SPF, DKIM and DMARC to enforcement without losing mail; DNSSEC; registry lock
  • Registrar and DNS migrations with no downtime
  • Selling: pricing, landing pages and marketplace listings for names you own
  • Website builds: fast, accessible, ad-ready sites on domains you already hold, with every item on our launch checklist done

Questions people ask first

Do you work with individuals or only companies?
Both. Many requests come from founders and small businesses with one important domain.
Will the owner know it’s us?
No. Acquisition outreach is anonymous unless you ask us to name you.
Is the first plan really free?
Yes. You get a written plan and a quote. You decide whether to go ahead.
How do you get paid on acquisitions?
A research fee agreed upfront and a success fee if the domain is yours. Payment for the domain itself goes through escrow, never through us.
